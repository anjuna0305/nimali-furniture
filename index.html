Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Built to Last

At Nimali Furniture, we aim to "Elevate your space with elegance" through an extensive collection of contemporary furniture, homeware products, and decorative items. Staying true to our promise and legacy, we are dedicated to designing and crafting pieces that offer the perfect blend of functionality, aesthetics, and comfort-ideally suited to complement Sri Lanka's tropical lifestyle. As a lifestyle brand, we are inspired to promote elegant, contemporary living by offering high-quality products paired with exclusive designs.

Our interior design and project division takes pride in bringing spaces to life through custom-designed furniture, carefully tailored to meet the unique needs of our diverse clientele-ranging from homeowners and apartment dwellers to commercial project developers. Every purchase from Nimali Furniture contributes to a greater purpose: supporting and sustaining the livelihoods of skilled local artisans and designers who are an integral part of our brand's journey.
